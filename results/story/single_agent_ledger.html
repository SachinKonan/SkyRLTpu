The elite-slots investigation

What elite slots actually did

Elite ∈ {0, 2, 8} was varied — but never as a single knob: it moved together with batch shape (64×8 arms ran elite 8, 8×64 arms elite ≤2). The verified findings:

  • Elite reshapes the 64×8 tree: it replaces consecutive polish chains with gap-tolerant reseeding — distelite15's champion is a depth-9 chain with 2–3-step gaps whose resumption parents ranked 118–242 under pure PUCT (never sampled without elite). Its run best came uniquely from an elite seed.
  • Elite is redundant once the pool collapses: counterfactually disabling it changes the seed set in 0/35 batches at 8×64 (vs 9/15 at 64×8-e8); elite-child advantage shrinks 0.0054 → 0.0003.
  • The famous "8×64 is 2–3× faster" is confounded with elite (shape and elite moved together), and the retracted sub-claim "elite produces 45–73% of top-decile children" was downgraded — at 8×64 it merely relabels PUCT's own picks.
  • Never run: 64×8-elite2 and 8×64-elite8, the two cells that would factorize shape from elite. Since Stage A the program pinned elite 2 for GRPO cells (0 for canonical TTD, later 2 in the matched-compute arms) and stopped varying it.

Era 4 · Aug 20

The grading bug: reported vs. tested performance

The grader ran its verifier, discarded the result, and returned the program's self-reported C₅ — guarded only by np.isclose(atol=1e-4), ~150× the margin separating records. A program could carry 0.02% excess mass, score the un-normalized correlate, and bank a fictional number. Consequences, all verified bit-for-bit:

  • tsw-n (tree-swap qwen control): 94/596 fictional states — killed.
  • g-grpo-n: published 0.380909993 → true 0.380917550; its best-claimed state was fictional and unreachable, so best-so-far froze for 7 of 15 steps. The strict-grader rerun (identical knobs) improved 13/14 steps and finished 0.380911409 — contamination didn't just mis-report, it halted search.
  • Six other completed cells carried 1–7 fictional states, never at the top — headlines unaffected.

Fixed in discover ae0a9bb: score the recomputed value, archive the projected density; verifier gates on sum(h)=n/2 and reports signed deltas.
