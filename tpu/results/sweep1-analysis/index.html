Entropic vs. GRPO on Qwen3.5‑27B

Five open mathematical-discovery problems, two advantage estimators, fifteen steps each. Three head-to-heads finished. None of them separates the estimators — but the runs disagree about almost everything else.

32 groups × 16 rollouts LoRA r32 · lr 4e‑5 KL 0 · ctx 18432 elite 2 · temp 1.0 PUCT pool, 5 416 pool programs analysed

00 The verdict

Each arm's champion in the problem's own units, with the direction of "better" spelled out. The number that decides whether a difference is real is beside it: the mean absolute step-to-step change in that run's per-step best — how much the run's output moves between two adjacent steps for no reason other than sampling.

Gap-to-swing is the champion gap divided by the two arms' mean step-to-step swing. All three completed comparisons land between 0.13 and 0.46 — the gap is a fraction of what a single step moves on its own. Nominally GRPO leads 2 of 3; that lead is inside the noise floor.

01 What actually ran

GCS is now the only source: the TPU slices are deleted. Gaps below are real gaps and are drawn as breaks in every line — nothing is interpolated across them.

Both Erdős arms restarted during step 0, so their pools contain no generation 0 — puct/buffer_size is still 32 at step 1 in both. They therefore have 14 productive generations against JSSP's 15. ttd-erdos is missing the metrics row for step 11 (slice preempted mid-step) but its pool entries for that generation survived, so pool-derived series are continuous there while metric-derived series break. ttd-ud lost its metrics.jsonl entirely; only its pool remains.

04 Improvement against the remaining headroom

Raw improvement must shrink as a run approaches an optimum, so it is not evidence of anything on its own. Two controls: parent-relative headroom divides each Δ by the distance from that child's own parent to the known target — the honest control — and fixed headroom divides by the distance the run faced at the very start, which keeps the denominator constant.

06 Group variance — does the step produce a gradient at all?

A step only trains if rewards inside a group differ. frac_mixed is the share of groups with mixed rewards; in these runs it is binary — every step is either fully mixed or fully dead. Only JSSP ever went dead, so the left panel shows JSSP alone; Erdős, AC1 and Circle sit at 1.0 on every logged step. Advantage spread (max − min) is what the estimator does with that variance.

09 Convergence

A step "improves" only if it moves the champion by more than a relative 1e‑9 — that threshold discards two float-noise gains (4e‑16 and 2e‑16 in grpo-acineq, 4e‑14 in ttd-circle) that would otherwise be counted as progress.
